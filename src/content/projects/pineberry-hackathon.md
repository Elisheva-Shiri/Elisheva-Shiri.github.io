## The hackathon

At the **International Startup Week** at **HNU** (Hochschule Neu-Ulm, Germany) in May 2023,
international teams had a few days to answer a real company's challenge. Our team won **first
place**.

## The challenge

The client is a **German–Kenyan energy platform** that plans and builds solar microgrids. After a
project is installed, customers keep coming back with the same questions, and the support team
spends hours answering them.

## PineBerry

**PineBerry** is an **AI assistant** for after-sales support. It is built on a knowledge base of
frequent questions, and it drafts answers that staff can select and adapt. A friendly pineapple
**avatar** gives it a face, and it speaks several languages, which none of the competitors offered.

We mapped the consumer journey, from research and consultation to project design, implementation
and maintenance, sketched every screen on paper, built a prototype, and pitched it to the jury.
