## The game

A game about making. Players follow **instructions to build figures out of recycled materials**:
old keyboards, cans and cartons.

## From waste to figure

1. **Take it apart.** Open old electronics such as keyboards, find the hidden screws and save the
   parts: panels, membranes and rubber domes.
2. **Build segments.** Cut and fold cans and cartons into small segments.
3. **Join them.** String the segments into a jointed figure.
4. **Bring it to life.** Hang the figure from a control board, and servo motors move its joints
   into new poses.
