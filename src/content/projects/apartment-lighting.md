## The design

A lighting plan for a large, high-end apartment in Tel Aviv. We used many kinds of light:
**uplights**, such as floor LEDs and lamps that create grounded scenes, and **downlights**, such
as chandeliers and wall luminaires that lift the atmosphere.

- **Work areas.** Cooler, bluer light around the office, the study and the reading corner.
- **Corridors.** High light levels on the routes to those areas, so it's easy to move around.
- **Feel.** Round luminaires that look like clouds give a calm, welcoming mood in the rooms and
  on the balconies.

## The calculation

The apartment was modelled in **DIALux evo** with real luminaires, mostly from Artemide, plus
Ateljé Lyktan, FLOS and others. Daylight for Tel Aviv was included, and light levels were
calculated room by room. The full report is 566 pages long.

Made with Dean Plobar, at Shenkar.
