## The kit

Children in hospital spend a lot of time waiting. This **fold-out kit** gives them something to do
with their hands: a booklet with pages to fill in, a pen and crayons held by elastic bands, a sheet of
round stickers, and a **3D-printed medal** to earn at the end.
