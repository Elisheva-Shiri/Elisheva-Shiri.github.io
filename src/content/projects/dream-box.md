## The installation

**Dream Box** is an outdoor light installation. **Addressable LED strips** run in lines across tall
chipboard walls, fanning out from a weatherproof control box at the base of each wall. At night
the strips light up with moving colour.

The strips were first tested at home, coiled on the floor, then wired on site in the field.
