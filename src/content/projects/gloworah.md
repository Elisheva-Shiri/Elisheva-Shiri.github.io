## The story

Gloworah is based on a design by **Evyatar Lior**. It is a tribute to the hanukkiah of his
childhood, which stood at the edge of his family's roof. Every year it was put up again, and
every year it connected the family with the people around them.

## A chain of light

To light a candle on Gloworah, you connect it with an electric cable to a neighbouring
hanukkiah. The cable stands for the connections within the network of people present. The first
candle is lit alone. As the days go by, we call on neighbours and family to join, building a
chain of light.

## Meaning

The endless light of the LED stands for an inexhaustible source of strength, a symbol of our
people and its long tradition. Together, the sharp forms, the black colour and the missing parts of
the structure represent the absence we live with today. They remind us of those who are not here,
and who will yet return.

## Making it

The hanukkiah's body is a custom circuit board carrying its LEDs. It stands in a holder modelled
in SolidWorks and 3D printed. The design went from breadboard prototypes to a finished board, then
to a series of units that can be wired together.
