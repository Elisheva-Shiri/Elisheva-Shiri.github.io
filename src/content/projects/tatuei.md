## The piece

**Tatuei** is a decoration of light. Hundreds of small iridescent pieces are arranged in a spiral
inside a 1.5-metre frame, standing on two posts staked into the ground. A single bulb on its own pole
lights it at night, and every piece throws back a bit of rainbow, so the spiral seems to turn.

Built for Midburn 2025.
