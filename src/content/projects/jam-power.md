## The challenge

A **one-week hackathon** (Jam Week) on human needs, run with the design-thinking and human-centred
design method. Our challenge was **loneliness**: an unpleasant emotional response that pushes
people to seek connection.

## Understanding the user

We built an **empathy map** and a persona: a student who is always on the go, loves to swim and
go out with friends, and needs to stay in control, stay close to friends and feel comfortable
being herself. Research showed that exercise opens opportunities for wider social networks and
social cohesion, and that Generation Z is always connected and always charging.

## The idea

**Jam Power** is a place to jam: to meet people, be active and do sport, where the energy of
your movement **charges your phone** and other devices. Instead of sitting alone next to a wall
socket, you plug in, move and connect.
