## The challenge

> How could we help recently moved first-year students, who avoid social media because of
> privacy fears, turn a superficial connection into a deep one, without too much effort?

A **one-week Jam Week** with NHL Stenden (the Netherlands) and Shenkar, on **loneliness in
Generation Z**. Gen Z spends a lot of time in closed places and connects mainly through screens,
which brings emotional distress, anxiety and a sense of separation. Research shows that physical
activity helps with anxiety, depression, mood and self-esteem.

## Understanding the user

We followed human-centred design. We built an empathy map and a persona, **Nicky Parker**, a
student who is good at meeting new people but doesn't know how to deepen the connection. That is
especially hard for her after moving to a new place.

## The design

**ELCO** is a space where users exercise together with an emphasis on **eye contact**. All
devices are made for two people who face each other and help each other. A screen next to each
pair shows their progress, their reward points and suggested topics of conversation.

The machines connect to a **mobile app**: you log in, invite friends, find workout partners and
call them to exercise with you. The movement **generates electricity**, and the points can be
spent on charity, **charging your phone** on the spot, or special lights on the screens.

Next steps: equipment for people with disabilities, equipment for groups larger than two, and
cooperation with environmental organisations.

Made with Dvir, Mandy, Niv and Thomas. We got feedback from Jan-Wessel Hovingh (UI/UX) and
Dr. Ximena Schmidt (sustainability and circular economy).
