## The project

In January 2018, in **Goa, India**, we cleaned a beach covered in plastic waste. The before and
after photos show the same stretch of sand.

Alongside the clean-up, a community event on the beach carried a giant **Earth balloon** along
the shore, a reminder of what the clean-up was for.
