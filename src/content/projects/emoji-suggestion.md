## The idea

Emojis are how we show feelings in digital dialogue, but the familiar reactions are limited. A
**chilli** already means a lot in our chats:

- **Hot, spicy**
- **Amazing, excited, happy**
- **Get away, don't touch**

This proposal, made for a course exercise on emotion in **digital dialogues**, turns the chilli into
a family of **reaction emojis with faces**, each colour and expression for a different feeling, to
bring some life to the conversation.
