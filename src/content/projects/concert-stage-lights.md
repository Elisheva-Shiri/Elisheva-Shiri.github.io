## The piece

A lighting decoration for the front of the stage at **Ayal's concert show**. A row of clear,
cut-out **boots** stands along the stage edge, and a single **LED strip** runs from one boot to the
next, outlining each one so the whole row glows in shifting purple, pink and blue.
