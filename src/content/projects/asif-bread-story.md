## The installation

“Bread Story” at **Asif** (Lilienblum 40, Tel Aviv) tells the story of bread in pictures: the
field, the wheat, the millstone, the flour and the loaf. The photographs are mounted on a tall
vertical strip that hangs by a column.

## The mechanism

A **stepper motor**, tested first on a driver board from a laptop, drives the strip through
**3D-printed gears**, so the story moves in front of the visitor.
