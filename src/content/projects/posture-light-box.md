## The idea

How much of what we see in a body depends on the light? **Changing Posture** places drawings of
human figures inside a white box lit by **RGB LEDs**. The drawings are made in **colour layers**,
so each light colour picks out a different set of lines, the muscles, the veins or the outline,
and the figure seems to move into a new posture as the light changes.
