## The problem

There is no simple way to connect people who want to volunteer with the people and places that
need help. We set a goal: to cut the share of people who don't volunteer because they can't find
the information from 33% to 15%. The 33% comes from Israel's Central Bureau of Statistics, 2018.

## Asking why

We asked "why?" three times:

1. **Why don't people volunteer?** They haven't heard of a place that suits them.
2. **Why?** Most places ask for a long-term commitment, and people have little free time.
3. **Why?** In uncertain times, people's schedules keep changing.

## Validation

We tested the problem with a validation board and a survey of 65 people, then brainstormed with
SCAMPER and looked at existing solutions. The most relevant were Facebook groups.

## The solution

A **social platform** with a light, easy interface that builds a network of volunteering in the
private and public sectors. It offers short, flexible opportunities, user profiles and
recommendations, and a "generous volunteer" ranking with small thank-you gifts from companies.
It's a **social business**: free for volunteers, and paid by the organisations, businesses and
municipalities that receive the help.

Made with Chen Golzman and Tomer Paldi in a course on the foundations of entrepreneurship and
management.
