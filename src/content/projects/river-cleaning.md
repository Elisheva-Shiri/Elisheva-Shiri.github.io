## The river

The Yarkon is one of the most polluted rivers in Israel. Rubbish thrown on its banks ends up in
the water: bottles, plastic bags and packaging. The project asks how a game could teach people,
especially children, to keep it clean.

## The game

Rubbish keeps floating up the river. **Click a bottle or a bag and it turns into a fish** that swims
away, earning 10 points. Leave the rubbish alone and it piles up at the top of the river. Reach 500
points to win.

## The idea

The game's main mechanic is the message itself: **removing rubbish brings life back**. Players
don't just collect points. They watch the river change, one fish at a time.

Python and pygame. The screenshots on this page were captured from the game's own code.
