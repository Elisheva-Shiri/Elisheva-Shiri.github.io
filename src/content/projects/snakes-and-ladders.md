## The game

Snakes and ladders, built upwards. Instead of one flat board, there are **three boards stacked
into a tower**. The ladders and snakes carry players between floors.

## How it was made

1. **Cardboard prototype.** Three boards made from boxes and a cardboard ladder, to test the idea.
2. **Mechanism.** The boards sit on **turntable bearings**, so they can rotate. A motor in a
   3D-printed holder drives them through **printed gears**.
3. **3D-printed build.** The boards, columns and gears were modelled, sliced in FlashPrint and
   printed. The holes in each board hold the playing pieces.
4. **Electronics.** Wires run from the boards to a controller under the tower.
