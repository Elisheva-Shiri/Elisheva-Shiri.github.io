## The murals

From 2017 to 2018 I painted murals wherever there was a wall: on houses, along staircases,
around pillars and on wooden beams. They show figures and faces, animals, water and forests,
often framed by geometric patterns and bright colour.

See also the [Pillar Mural](/projects/pillar-mural/), painted on all four sides.
