## The project

An immersive environment for a restaurant, designed through light. The room is planned so that
light wraps the diners: warm and cool cones over each table, glowing fittings along the ceiling
and softer light in the quieter areas.

## From sketch to model

1. **Sketches.** Chalk on black paper, drawing light instead of objects: where it falls, its
   colour, and the shadows it leaves.
2. **Model.** The restaurant was built in **DIALux evo**, with real luminaires loaded from
   manufacturers' photometric files (IES/ULD), and the light levels were calculated.
