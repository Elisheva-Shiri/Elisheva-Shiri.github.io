## The project

An immersive environment for a restaurant, designed through light. Each area has its own
mood:

- **Dining rooms.** Wall lights cast patterns on the grey walls, over bright pink and red
  chairs.
- **Green plant walls.** They separate the rooms.
- **Bar.** Lit by a glowing wall of **water light**.
- **Quiet corners.** Softer pendant and floor lamps.

## From sketch to model

1. **Sketches.** Chalk on black paper, drawing the light instead of objects: where it falls, its
   colour, and the shadows it leaves.
2. **Model.** The restaurant was built in **DIALux evo**, with real luminaires from
   manufacturers' photometric files (IES/ULD). I calculated the light levels on the working planes
   of the dining area and the restrooms.

This was exercise 1 of the **Optical Design** course in the Electrical Engineering department at
Shenkar. See also the [office](/projects/office-lighting/), [theatre](/projects/theater-lighting/)
and [apartment](/projects/apartment-lighting/) designs from the same course.
