**Gerev** (Ayal Regev) is a rapper whose work mixes rap and tech. For the launch of
his debut album, _First Timer_, I designed the art installations for the show together with
**Merav Veder**. The show was held on
**7 January 2026 in Tel Aviv, Israel**.

## Concept

_Gerev_ means **sock** in Hebrew, so the whole show was built around socks. The theme ran
through every part of the space: the stage, the screens and the venue itself.

## The stage

The stage featured **eight socks**, each with its own design: dollar signs, circuit boards,
rockets, bees on a honeycomb, social-media icons, flames, clouds and a clean sock carrying a
small sock emblem. Their sequences changed with the music, so the stage moved through the
set together with the songs.

## Video art

Alongside the stage, video art filled the screens with socks raining down and piling up.
The clips were slowed down so their motion matched the pace of the show.

## Fabrication

The hardware that held the installation together, including curved brackets, arms, a frame
and mounting plates, was modelled in SolidWorks and 3D printed.

## Socks everywhere

Beyond the stage, the entire venue was filled with socks, turning the album launch into one
continuous sock-themed environment.

## Timeline

From concept to finished installation, the project was designed and built in
**18 working hours**.
