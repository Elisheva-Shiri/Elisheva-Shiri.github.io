## The build

A new piece for the Monster Theater. Three large **arcade buttons**, one red and two yellow, sit
in a 3D-printed box. Long cables, sleeved in fabric, connect it to a separate control unit.

The box was modelled, printed in parts, joined, and then wired by hand.
