## The experience

You stand in the middle. Around you is the sea: sound, light and space all around and above you.
You don't have to do anything. You can't swim anywhere or complete a task. You can only listen,
look around and be underwater.

## Sound first

The experience is built around **sound**. The movement and muffled hush of water surround the
visitor from every side, so that being "inside" the sea is something you hear as much as see.

## Built with

Unity, for the Meta Quest headset.
