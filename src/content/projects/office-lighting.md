## The project

An immersive environment for an office. Work areas need bright, even light (on average around
500 lux on the desks), while the meeting rooms, kitchen and green areas around the central
meeting table can be softer and warmer.

## From sketch to model

The light was first sketched in chalk on black paper, then the office was modelled in
**DIALux evo** with luminaires from manufacturers' photometric files.
