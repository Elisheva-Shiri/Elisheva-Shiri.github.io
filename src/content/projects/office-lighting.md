## The project

An immersive environment for an office. Work areas need bright, even light, around 500 lux on the
desks. The meeting rooms, the lounge and the green areas can be softer and warmer. Tall green
plant walls frame quiet **focus booths**.

## From sketch to model

The light was first sketched in chalk on black paper. Then the office was modelled in **DIALux
evo**, with luminaires from manufacturers' photometric files, and the light was calculated over
the working areas.

This was exercise 1 of the **Optical Design** course at Shenkar. See also the
[restaurant](/projects/immersive-restaurant/), [theatre](/projects/theater-lighting/) and
[apartment](/projects/apartment-lighting/) designs.
