## The mural

A pillar painted on all four sides. A **green dragon** winds around it, between blue clouds and
orange strokes. Each side shows a different figure: one **meditating** in a yellow sun, one
**dancing** on an orange ball, and one **seated** with raised arms. Blue geometric patterns and
red and yellow bands frame the figures above and below.
