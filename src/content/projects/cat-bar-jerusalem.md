## The project

The **Cat Bar** in Jerusalem was renovated in 2016 and reopened in March 2017. Before the
reopening night, a teaser campaign built up the excitement, from cat eyes glowing in the dark
with “Something new is coming…” to a countdown, “3 days to go”.
