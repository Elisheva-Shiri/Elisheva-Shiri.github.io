## 2021: the moulds

Self-made moulds for a creative art piece for the **Cat Kingdom**. The pieces are cast in **egg
cartons**, so every cup becomes a mould. Each piece has a hollow centre, and a string of
**fairy lights** is threaded through them so they glow.

## 2025: flowers, rocks and light

Four years later, a new set of pieces for the Cat Kingdom: **felt flowers**, soft **rock forms**
covered in white fabric, and a **radial light sculpture** that casts a pattern of rays around it.
Everything was made in the workshop and then taken out to the desert.
