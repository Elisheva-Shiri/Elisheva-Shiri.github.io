## The game

A shape-sorting game for the **TOM conference**. There are three shapes, a triangle, a circle and
a hexagon. Each is split into pieces that fit together, and then into a matching slot in the
tray.

The shapes were modelled in **Fusion 360** and 3D-printed in bright colours, with a red tray.
