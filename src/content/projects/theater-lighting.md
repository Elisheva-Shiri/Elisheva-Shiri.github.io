## The project

A theatre hall prepared for lighting design. The plan was brought from **AutoCAD** into
**AGI32**, and a grid of calculation points was laid over the stage and the seating, ready for
light levels to be calculated once the luminaires are placed.

Part of the **Optical Design** course at Shenkar. See also the
[restaurant](/projects/immersive-restaurant/), [office](/projects/office-lighting/) and
[apartment](/projects/apartment-lighting/) designs.
