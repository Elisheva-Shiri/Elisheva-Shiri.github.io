## The question

Our hands have been shaped by the tools we use, from stone tools to smartphones. **What if they
kept changing?** This project imagines hands with different fingers and tests how each version
would change something as simple as opening a jar of sweets.

## The experiment

I modelled finger extensions and caps in SolidWorks and 3D printed them, one for each finger,
plus two-finger pieces that merge neighbouring fingers. Wearing them, I tried the same everyday
task again and again:

- **Capped fingertips:** a subtle change to touch and grip.
- **Long fingers:** reaching and pinching change completely.
- **Merged fingers:** the hand becomes a single rounded form that can push but hardly grasp.

## Connection

The experiment grew out of the research for [JULIET-SRR: The Glove](../juliet-srr/), which asked
how technology may deform our hands in the future.
