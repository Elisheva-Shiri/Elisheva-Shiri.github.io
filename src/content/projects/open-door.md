## The installation

**Open Door** (in Hebrew-Arabic wordplay, *Yiftach El-Bab*) is a door standing alone in the desert.
Its frame opens onto a **mandala of light**: LED strips driven by a custom controller board, so the
door glows at night and invites people to step through.

Built for Midburn 2025. More details to come.
