## The installation

**Open Door** (in Hebrew-Arabic wordplay, *Yiftach El-Bab*) is a door standing alone in the desert.
Its panel glows from behind, and on it sit two cut-out **spiral mandalas**, one over the other.
Turn the handle at the side, and a **planetary gear train** turns the spirals against each other,
so their overlap keeps changing into new patterns of colour and shadow.

## The mechanism

The gears and spirals were drawn, laser-cut from plywood and painted. A ring gear with planet gears
around a sun gear sits on a wooden board, and every axle is a stack of spacers, a bearing, washers
and a nut on a bolt through the board, sketched first by hand. The panel is backlit by LED strips
driven by a custom controller board.

Built for Midburn 2025.
