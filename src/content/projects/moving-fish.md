## The object

A small kinetic sculpture. A fish cut from orange and yellow felt is mounted on a bent wire arm.
A **DC motor** turns a **gear** that rocks the arm, so the fish swims back and forth above its
base. **RGB LEDs** inside the base glow through it and shift colour as it moves.

The base, motor and switches were assembled by hand on a white round plate.
